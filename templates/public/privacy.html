1. {% trans "Who is responsible for your data" %}

{% blocktrans %}The data controller for this service is the Louis WP Scan project. If you have any question about this policy or about your data, you can contact us at contact@louiswpscan.org.{% endblocktrans %}

2. {% trans "What we collect and why" %}

{% trans "We follow the principle of data minimisation. We only collect what is needed to run the service." %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Scan data. When you scan a website we store the address you entered, the date and time of the scan, and a short summary of the result, such as the number of issues found and the security grade. We do not store the full content of your website or any login details.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Contact data. If you use the contact form, we store the name, email address, subject and message you provide, so that we can reply to you.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Analytics data. With your consent only, we use Google Analytics to understand how the service is used so we can improve it. This may include anonymised usage data such as pages visited and approximate region.{% endblocktrans %}

3. {% trans "Legal basis for processing" %}

  • {% blocktrans %}Legitimate interest for running the scan you requested and keeping basic summary records to operate and improve the service.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Consent for analytics cookies. These are switched off until you accept them, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.{% endblocktrans %}
  • {% blocktrans %}Consent and legitimate interest for replying to messages you send us through the contact form.{% endblocktrans %}

4. {% trans "Cookies and analytics" %}

{% blocktrans %}We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies that are required for the website to work, for example to keep your chosen language and to protect forms against abuse. These do not require consent.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}We also use Google Analytics, but only after you click Accept on our cookie banner. Until then, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics scripts are loaded. You can change your decision at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.{% endblocktrans %}
