# Louis WP Scan

> Louis WP Scan is a free, non profit service that scans WordPress websites for common security weaknesses and gives a clear, step by step guide to fix them. No account is needed, and the scan only reads public pages. The project is named in tribute to the physicist Louis de Broglie.

## About
- Mission: protect small businesses and everyday people from hacking and the illegal use of their data, by making practical security knowledge free for everyone.
- Free and non profit, available in English and French.
- The scan is safe and read only. It never logs in, never changes anything, and never runs a real brute force or denial of service test.

## What the scanner checks
- Login and brute force exposure (including default usernames)
- Default URLs such as wp-admin, and default files
- XML-RPC abuse used for password guessing and DDoS
- DDoS and web application firewall readiness
- HTTPS, security headers, and WordPress version disclosure
- Username enumeration and directory listing
